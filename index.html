How this works. Locked is the points each team has locked so far (what Sleeper's app shows). Proj is each team's expected final if they make the best calls from here: their locks so far plus the lineup the board would set for them, using the same projections and lock/wait math as the Lock-in tab. Locks come straight from Sleeper when your login is saved. Without it, the public data only proves a lock once that player's next game starts, so other teams show "at least" that many. Playoff odds come from simulating the rest of the regular season (through week 21) 8,000 times. This week uses the same score simulation as the matchup cards (real score shapes, locks so far, the best calls from here), so its win and median odds match the scoreboard; later weeks use team strength: the best weekly lineup from each roster's projected players, scaled to this league's real scoring level and blended 70/30 with the points each team has actually scored. Each run also redraws how good every team really is (about ±10 points a week, for injuries, trades and projection misses), so a strong start doesn't read as a lock. Each week counts twice, the head-to-head plus the game against the league median, and the top 8 by record (then points) make the playoffs. The playoffs are simulated too (8 teams, 1v8 / 4v5 / 2v7 / 3v6, no byes, one week per round) to get title odds.

Sleeper login & how it works

Paste your Sleeper login and the board reads every team's locks straight from Sleeper. It's saved only in this browser and sent only to sleeper.com. Anyone who has it can act as you on Sleeper, so only use it on your own devices.

To find it: log in at sleeper.com in Chrome, open DevTools (⌥⌘I) → Network, click any request named graphql, and copy the authorization value under Request Headers.

Lock or wait. After each game a starter's score can be locked or replaced by his next game; his last game counts if it's never locked. The board works out what waiting is worth with your bench as backup, then picks whichever gives better championship odds. Those come from simulating the rest of the season and the playoffs, where this week's head-to-head and league-median results each count as a win and points break ties.

        
Your opponent is assumed to play for their own title odds too, and to react to your locks. Worst case is your win chance if they ignore the median and gamble everything on beating you.

        
Pickups clear overnight, so a claim counts from tomorrow. Only Out players are allowed in IR here, and anyone else there blocks pickups until he's activated.
