How to use this board
  • Pick your team in the team menu at the top of the Matchup tab. The board remembers it.
  • Top of the Matchup tab: both teams' projected totals, your chance to win the matchup, to beat the league median (and the score that should take), and to win the title.
  • To do lists every move in order with times: who to start or bench, and "after X's game: lock it if he scores Y+".
  • Each row is a lineup slot. Lock: lock that score (by the time in To do). Wait: keep playing him. "lock at Y+": the score worth locking. "→ Name": the plan gives the slot to that player.
  • Tap a player for his games, projection, and the odds of locking vs playing on.
  • League tab: every matchup (tap one for both lineups and each team's lock odds), standings with playoff and title odds, and how each manager's past lock calls went.
  • A player shows Locked only once Sleeper confirms it, which can take about 10 minutes.
  • It's a guide, not a rule: when the two options are close, either is fine.
